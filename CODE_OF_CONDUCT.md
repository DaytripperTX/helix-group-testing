# Code Of Conduct

Helix Group Testing contributors and admins are expected to keep project work
private, respectful, and focused on safe site operation.

## Expectations

- Protect private project information, credentials, and admin-only workflows.
- Discuss security concerns privately instead of exposing exploitable details.
- Keep reviews direct, useful, and respectful.
- Avoid adding claims, workflows, or public copy that conflict with the site's
  disclaimers and non-commercial scope.
- Respect the proprietary license and do not reuse the project for unrelated or
  competing work.

Project access may be removed for behavior that puts the site, its admins, or
its users at risk.
